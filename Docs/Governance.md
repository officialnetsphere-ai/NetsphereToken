# NETSPH Governance

NETSPH may support governance-related participation within the Netsphere ecosystem where the platform provides such features.

## Governance Scope

Governance may include community feedback or voting on:

- Ecosystem priorities
- Partner campaign direction
- Community incentive programs
- Platform feature proposals
- Treasury-related recommendations where applicable

Governance features may be introduced gradually and may depend on technical readiness, security review, and platform policy.

Governance participation does not guarantee financial returns, ownership rights, dividends, or control over Netsphere entities.
