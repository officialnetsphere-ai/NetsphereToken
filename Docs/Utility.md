# NETSPH Utility

NETSPH is designed for use within the Netsphere ecosystem.

## Core Utilities

- Platform access
- Ecosystem rewards
- Community incentives
- Partner campaign participation
- Loyalty and engagement programs
- Governance participation where supported
- Fee discounts or ecosystem benefits where available
- Staking-related campaign eligibility, subject to future rules

## Rewards Notice

Rewards are utility-based incentives within the Netsphere ecosystem. They are not guaranteed returns, dividends, interest, fixed income, or investment income.

Reward availability depends on platform rules, campaign terms, technical readiness, and applicable laws.
